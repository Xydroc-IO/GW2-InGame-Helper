Mist Potions

Fractal-specific potions from the Mist Potions vendor / crafting. Use one offensive stack.

PotionUse
Mist Offensive Potion Default DPS pick — outgoing damage in fractals
Mist Defensive Potion Tank / rough CMs — incoming damage reduction
Mist Mobility Potion Skip-heavy or movement-heavy scales (situational)
Integrated / account potions If you own combined/infinite variants, use those instead of buying stacks

Tip: Offensive is the usual T4 / CM default. Defensive is fine on tank or when learning a CM.

Survivability — Agony Resistance

AR prevents instant death from the Agony damage-over-time effect. Comes from infusions (+ enrichment).

TierScalesMinimum ARCommunity / recommended
T11–250 (Agony starts at scale 20)—

CM95–100 mote+150Same as T4 minimum
  • T2 — Agony appears from scale 20; you need resistance before entering T2. Min +18 at 26; +61 recommended for the whole tier.
  • Infusions — socket into ascended/legendary gear. Recycle via mystic forge / vendors when upgrading.
  • +9 agony — endgame fractal standard; full slots approach the +162 community target.
  • Enrichment — +5 AR jewelry enrichments help early AR goals.
