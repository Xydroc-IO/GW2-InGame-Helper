Raid Utilities

Enhancement (utility) picks by role — oils, sharpening stones, focusing crystals, and enrichment.

Enhancement Enrichment Notes

Enhancement by Role

Slot in the utility / enhancement consumable bar (30 min). Pair with feast food from Raid Food.

RoleCommon pickWhat it gives
Power Superior Sharpening Stone Power + Ferocity
Condition Toxic Focusing Crystal Condition Damage + Expertise
Heal Bountiful Maintenance Oil Healing Power + Concentration
Boon DPS / Support Enhanced Lucent Oil or Furious Tuning Crystal Concentration-focused (Lucent) or Power/Precision/Ferocity (Furious) — match the build
Tank Hardened Sharpening Stone or Bountiful Maintenance Oil Toughness + Power, or Healing/Concentration if the tank heals

Also fine: Master Tuning Crystal (condi), Peppermint Oil (heal/support seasonal), Potent variants when you have them. Feasts already cover nourishment — don’t double-stack the same food type.

Enrichment (Ascended Jewelry)

One enrichment per ascended accessory / ring / amulet (account-bound upgrades).

RoleTypical enrichment
Power Mighty / Precise / Malign (+attribute) — often Power or Precision; some use Ferocity
Condition Malign / Raven / Expertise-leaning picks matching the build’s condition attribute
Heal / Quick / Alac Concentration enrichments (or Healing Power if the build is heal-stacked)
Tank Toughness or Concentration depending on whether you need more toughness or boon duration

Agony: Fractal / CM players often prefer +5 Agony Resistance enrichments on jewelry. Raids do not require AR — prioritize attributes for raid-only characters.

Quick Notes

Stacking and shopping reminders.

  • Food + utility — both should be up; feast nourishment + enhancement utility.
  • Shared feasts — one cook can cover the squad; utilities are personal.
  • WvW / open world — different oils/stones exist; this sheet is raid/strike oriented.
  • Build overrides — always follow your build page if it names a specific utility.
