Priority Order

Do the cheap impactful pieces first.

  1. Trinkets — Laurel / fractal / LS vendors (fast stats).
  2. Weapons — craft, fractal, or raid drops for your main sets.
  3. Armor — craft, fractal, or strike/raid — full set last if needed.
  4. Infusions — agony for fractals (see AR); attribute infusions later.

Trinkets

Usually the first ascended slots.

  • Laurels — rings / accessories / amulets from laurel vendors.
  • Fractal — rings / accessories with agony infusion slots.
  • Living World / expansion — map vendors and collections.
  • Enrichment — +5 AR on jewelry helps early fractal goals.
