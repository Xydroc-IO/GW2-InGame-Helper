Bloodstone · Empyreal · Dragonite

Ascended crafting triad.

  • Used together for ascended gear and many legendary gifts.
  • Convert excess via mystic forge boxes when storage is full.
  • Map completion, fractals, raids, and dailies drip these constantly.
