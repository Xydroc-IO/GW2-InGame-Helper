Breakbar Basics

Blue defiance bar = dump CC. Soft CC builds slowly; hard CC spikes.

TypeExamples
Hard CC Stun · Knockdown · Knockback · Launch · Float · Sink · Daze (strong) · Pull
Soft CC Immobilize · Cripple · Chill · Fear · Taunt · Slow

Save big CC for the bar. Don’t waste long cooldowns on empty bars.

Squad Tips

Coordination beats random CC.

  • Call the bar — “CC now” beats everyone holding or everyone dumping early.
  • Stuns stack poorly on immunity — after a break, many bosses go defiance-immune briefly.
  • Pulls / knocks — can ruin stack; know if the boss can be moved.
  • Special action key — some encounters give encounter CC (use it).
