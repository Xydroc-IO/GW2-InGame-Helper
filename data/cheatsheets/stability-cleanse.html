Squad Tips

Resistance ≠ cleanse

Resistance lowers condi damage taken; it does not remove conditions.

Convert vs purge

Some scourge / FB tools convert conditions instead of cleansing.

Stack first

Stab/cleanse on a spread squad wastes the button.

Pair sheets

Use with CC / Defiance and Boon Checklist.
