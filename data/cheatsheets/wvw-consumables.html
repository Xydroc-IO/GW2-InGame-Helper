Food & Utility

Bring what your build expects — raid food often still applies.

  • Food — power / condi / tank food matching your role (see Raid Food).
  • Utility — oils, tuning crystals, sharpening stones as for PvE (see Raid Utilities).
  • WvW-specific — siege / supply consumables from vendors when stocked.
  • Maintenance oil / crystals — same logic as open world / raids.
