Portals

Move the squad through gates and skips.

ProfessionTypical tools
MesmerPortal Entre / Exeunt — primary raid/fractal portal

Tip: One reliable mesmer portal covers most group content. Call portal before dropping it.

Pulls & Stacks

Group enemies or allies.

  • Necromancer — wells / spectral / scourge pulls (common trash clear).
  • Elementalist — cyclone / magnetic / staff tools on some builds.
  • Guardian / Revenant / Warrior — pulls on specific elite specs / utilities.
  • Call the pull — wait for boons / tank before yanking.

Reflects & Projectile hate

Block or return projectiles on key fights.

Guardian

Wall of Reflection · Shield of Absorption — classic reflect kit.

Mesmer

Feedback · mirror skills — bubble / reflect windows.

Elementalist

Arcane Shield / magnetic aura lines — situational.

Timing

Reflect on call — wasted early is a wipe later.
