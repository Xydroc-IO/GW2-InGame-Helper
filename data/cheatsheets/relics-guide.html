Guild Wars 2 · Relic Reference

Relics

Short “what to socket” sheet by role and damage type. One relic per build.

Power Condition Support Tank

Power

Crit / burst / on-tag relics common in raids and strikes.

  • Relic of Fireworks — classic on-crit damage; still a safe default for many power builds
  • Relic of the Thief / Eir / Dragonhunter — profession or rotation-gated power options
  • Relic of the Wizard's Tower / Leadership — when the build stacks might or specific triggers
  • Relic of the Warrior — banner / power-support hybrids

Condition

Condition application and duration oriented relics.

  • Relic of the Fractal — strong general condi default for many builds
  • Relic of Ahamkara / Peitha / Afflicted — condition-type specialists
  • Relic of the Nightmare — torment / fear oriented kits
  • Relic of the Astral Ward — when the build page calls for it

Heal / Quick / Alac

Healing and boon uptime relics.

  • Relic of the Monk — heal support staple
  • Relic of the Flock / Mercy / Karakosa — alternate heal procs
  • Relic of Leadership / Speed / Chronomancer — boon DPS / quick / alac variants
  • Relic of the Mirage — when playing mirage support lines

Tank

Survivability and group utility.

  • Relic of the Midnight King — common tank / toughness-oriented pick
  • Relic of Durability — mitigation focused
  • Heal-tank — often shares heal relics (Monk / Flock) instead

One relic only. Follow your build’s named relic when it disagrees with this sheet — balance patches move the goalposts.
