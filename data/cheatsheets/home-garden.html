Cultivated Herbs → Feast Utility

Matches the Universal Seasoning Guide on Raid Food.

PlantFeast utilityPriority

Cultivated Mint +10% Outgoing Healing High — heal / support desserts & plates
Cultivated Clove −20% Incoming Condition Duration Medium — condi-heavy fights
Cultivated Sesame Health Regeneration Medium — hybrid / openers

Lime & Divinity Fair Herbs still appear in recipes — garden herbs are the cultivated third ingredient that sets the utility.

Planting Tips

Home instance garden / homestead garden nodes.

  • Stock Cilantro + Peppercorn first — cover the two most requested feast utilities.
  • Mint — keep a row if you cook for healers / crème brûlée.
  • Harvest daily — cultivated plants are account-bound ingredients; grow more than you think you need before raid night.
  • Open Raid Food — full recipes & bases live on that sheet.

Also Useful in the Garden

Not seasonings, but common feast ingredients.

  • Sawgill / mushrooms — some clove steak variants
  • Butternut Squash — veggie flatbreads
  • Allium / garlic nodes — coq au vin & sauce bases (as available)
  • Channel Bound / other gardens — same cultivated herbs if your instance layout differs
